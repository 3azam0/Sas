Live at https://fodo.am-agent2026.workers.dev/?release=af2bb90d5fdbca37.

The butchery module is integrated with the current hosted application, preserving the kitchen document-price release (`d788ba0`). It processes live sheep (ضاني) through slaughter/cutting and purchased beef through cutting. Birds, goats and live cattle are outside this workflow. Users create or classify their own kilogram stock items; there are no fixed product/cut names or automatically seeded live stock.

Select the central kitchen/butchery stock location, receive or transfer inputs through the existing stock workflows, record actual weights and losses, and assign output cost percentages totaling 100. Sheep batches also record head count and usable carcass weight including kitchen-used organs. Outputs can move directly to main inventory or kitchen locations through existing transfers, including partial receipts and in-transit value. Costing uses the organization's FIFO or moving-average method and preserves exact total value. Accepted batch history retains inputs, outputs, losses, processing cost and each product's assigned cost.

The shared offline outbox, generation checks, identities, stock projections, count blockers, sync status and recovery format 22 include butchery. Kitchen pricing recovery format 21 remains supported.
