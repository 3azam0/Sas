# Period consumption reconciliation

Open **التقارير والتحليلات → مطابقة الاستهلاك**. Select the period and current/all locations; optionally select kitchen, category, material or search. A material/location remains one row, with its own base unit. Rows are sorted/paged; summaries and top-ten signed variance values use all filtered rows. CSV includes all pages, exact decimals, count identifiers and reasons. Printing includes the current page. Previously loaded queries can be reopened offline; pending commands are excluded until acceptance.

For a complete comparison, synchronize every device, pause stock activity and perform actual physical counts on the day before the period and its final day. Count business dates must match the Cairo date of physical capture. A backdated count or a manual **تعديل الرصيد** is not a substitute. Every material needs its own accepted opening and closing count line; partial counts certify only their counted materials. There is no automatic accounting period closure or guarantee that unseen offline operations have all arrived.

Expected usage uses every ingredient in each unique accepted sale's pinned recipe, stopping at stocked preparation boundaries. Selective deduction controls do not remove unchecked ingredients from theoretical usage. Actual consumption is opening physical quantity plus signed non-consumption movements minus closing physical quantity. Purchasing, returns, transfers, recorded waste and production inputs/outputs are classified separately. Independently, actual must equal recorded sales/legacy consumption minus physical count adjustments. A positive unexplained difference is extra consumption; it is not automatically classified as waste.

Missing or inconsistent count boundaries, late/backdated ledger entries, manual balance changes, unknown movements, unresolved recipes or a ledger/balance/layer discrepancy leave actual and variance blank, with the reason in the table. Recorded deductions and theoretical requirements remain visible. Sale shortages retain their accepted missing quantities and are never automatically backfilled. Shortages alone do not invalidate a proven physical count comparison; they still need operational investigation.

Both actual and theoretical quantities use the remaining-stock average reconstructed at the closing count. This common basis isolates quantity variance from changing prices and is an analytical valuation, not finalized sales COGS or net profit. A positive closing quantity with explicit zero value has valid zero cost. A zero closing quantity has no remaining-stock price, so cost is unavailable. Summary amounts cover only complete and priced rows, with coverage shown; kilograms, litres and counts are never added together.

The report is read-only: no stock posting, correction, shortage settlement, count approval, reset, schema migration or offline database upgrade. Financial corrections and restaurant expenses remain later slices.
