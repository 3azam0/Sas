# Direct multi-item receiving

Compact layout update, 15 October 2026: the date and **تفاصيل إضافية** control now sit on the left of the desktop form header and wrap beneath its title on phones. The optional supplier/reference still expand in the body and retain validation/focus behavior. Added-item cards use small inline quantity, cost and value pairs with content-driven height; edit/delete and purchase-unit conversion details remain visible. See [verified header/card release](../releases/receiving-header-cards-2026-10-15.md).

Usability update, 15 October 2026: start from the **استلام مواد** home action or the inventory/history header. The date stays visible; **تفاصيل إضافية** contains the optional supplier/reference and starts closed on new receipts. Filled optional values remain summarized when collapsed, and invalid fields are revealed for correction. The final action is now **حفظ الاستلام**. A document-specific notice distinguishes pending local capture, confirmation and review; it follows the saved operation rather than announcing stock acceptance after Save alone. The existing validation, stock posting and unit/cost rules below still apply.

1. Select the destination **الموقع**. The location and inventory units must exist; suppliers are optional. You can create a material inside receiving even when the catalog is empty.
2. Click **استلام مواد**. Choose a supplier or keep **بدون مورد محدد**, set the business date and optionally enter **مرجع الاستلام** (invoice number or note). A blank reference is allowed; a supplied reference must contain 2–200 characters. The system retains its unique document and line IDs in either case.
3. Use the single material picker and adjacent quantity input; choose a receiving unit/pack and cost **per base inventory unit**. For example, 2 bags × 5 kg = 10 kg; at 20 EGP/kg the line value is 200 EGP.

   **وحدة الاستلام** includes every unit registered in **إعداد المخزون → وحدات المخزون**, together with this item's fixed purchase packs. The base unit appears once as **وحدة المخزون**. kg/g and litre/ml aliases convert automatically: 500 g received into a kg-based item = 0.5 kg; at EGP 20/kg the value is EGP 10. For another unit, enter **محتوى وحدة الاستلام**: for example, 1 **عدد** of a bag contains 5 kg, so 2 bags = 10 kg. This factor belongs to this receipt; define a fixed item purchase pack in setup if the relationship should be reused. A litre-to-kg relationship also requires an explicit factor; density is never assumed. Changing the selected item/unit clears an unfinished custom factor. Add any missing named unit through inventory setup.

4. Press Enter after quantity to focus cost, then Enter after cost (or **إضافة إلى القائمة** / Alt+Insert) to add the completed entry and return to item search. The newest item appears at the top of a read-only receipt list. Clicking/selecting a material focuses quantity. Repeated items combine exactly only when cost, receiving unit and conversion factor match; otherwise edit the existing line or use a separate receipt. **تعديل** loads the line into the entry area and replaces its quantity/cost/unit when confirmed; cancelling preserves the existing line. **حذف** removes it and recalculates totals. Up to 200 unique items are allowed. Above ten items, search, sorting and ten-row pagination are available; the footer always totals all items. Adding/editing restores newest-first order and the first page.

   For a missing material, choose **إضافة مادة جديدة** at the bottom of the material dropdown or press **F2** in that field. Enter on a search with no matching items also opens creation. The name is prefilled from the search; provide a unique code, category and inventory unit, then click **حفظ واختيار**. The material starts at zero stock, is selected in the entry area, and quantity receives focus. Supplier, date, reference and the receipt list stays intact. **إلغاء** or Escape closes only the creation form and returns to the original material selection. Creation is saved independently: the catalog material remains if you later cancel the receipt. Additional purchase packs, minimum stock and recipe components can be edited through inventory setup afterward.
5. Review base quantities, line values and the document total. Add or clear any unfinished entry/edit before saving; it is never silently discarded. Then click **حفظ الاستلام**. All rows save together on this device, including offline. Confirmed stock changes only after the server accepts the whole document.
6. Open **مركز المزامنة** to see receipts from every location, including after a reload. **سجل الحركات** shows receipt documents and accepted line movements for the selected location.
7. Reconnect and synchronize. Repeated synchronization cannot post the same receipt twice. A rejected line rejects the whole receipt; no partial stock is posted. Retry the original document, correct it into a linked new document, or cancel the rejected document from its history. Accepted receipts cannot be edited/cancelled through this screen.

Receipt history and correction retain entered quantity, chosen unit, base-unit name and conversion factor. Converted quantities must fit six decimal places without rounding. Standard conversion ratios and tenant references are checked locally and on the server; an invalid row rejects the whole document. Unit-name changes after capture require correcting a pending document using current names; historical names/factors remain intact in accepted documents and recovery.

Use recovery export/import (current format 16) to preserve whole receipts, unit conversions and rejected correction history. Formats 1–13 remain readable subject to organization/generation checks; files containing the new catalog-unit metadata require version 14. Older Fodo builds cannot restore version 14. Setup uploads precede receipts. If a newly created material or a selected unit/location/supplier is not confirmed, the receipt stays saved for review; correct or synchronize setup before retrying. Unaccepted opening stock and rejected preceding receipts put dependent stock operations into review; resolve them before retrying.

Direct receiving is independent of purchase orders. Order receipts remain in **المشتريات** and use their existing captured pack definitions. Returns from direct receipts, tax/discount/freight allocation and supplier balances/payments are future work. The client's 328-item classified catalog is already imported; this unit extension does not import, edit or delete catalog data or add stock.


At desktop widths of 900px and above, the entry area appears on the right and the receipt list on the left, with separate scrolling and fixed total/Save actions. Document details collapse automatically after adding a material, leaving supplier/date/reference summarized; click **بيانات المستند** to reopen. Invalid document fields reopen before focus. Keyboard hints are in the compact fixed header.

On mobile, the entry remains above the list. Use **عرض القائمة** to jump to the added items, or **طي** beside **إضافة مادة** to free space without clearing an unfinished entry. Both entry and list can be expanded again. Adding/editing reopens the receipt list, returns it to the first page/top and highlights the latest changed item. Search receives focus after Add with its menu closed; typing, clicking and arrow keys still open results.
