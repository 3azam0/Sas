# Daily workflow

Delivered 15 October 2026. Choose the stock location, then use **استلام مواد**, **صرف للمطبخ** or **عرض المخزون** on **نظرة عامة**. Disabled actions explain the missing setup. **ما يحتاج انتباهك** links to low stock in the selected location and pending/review documents on this device across all locations. It does not claim to audit all business activity.

Desktop keeps daily operations visible and groups other screens under expandable sections. Opening a screen reveals its active group. On phones, the bottom bar holds overview, inventory, kitchens, sales and **القائمة**; the latter opens all 17 sections. Escape/close returns focus to its button. Changing screens returns the content to its top.

Receiving keeps its date visible; optional supplier/reference are under **تفاصيل إضافية للاستلام**. Kitchen supply keeps kitchen/date visible and puts optional reference/notes under **تفاصيل إضافية للصرف**. Existing values appear in the summary and are preserved while collapsed. Save opens and focuses invalid hidden fields. Item selection moves to quantity; Enter adds a complete line and returns to search. Added lines remain visible beside entry on desktop. In-app help retains the draft.

**حفظ الاستلام** and **حفظ صرف المطبخ** capture local documents. The resulting notice follows that operation's outbox state within its tenant/generation: saved locally, sent awaiting confirmed balance, confirmed stock update, needs review, or cancelled/superseded. Sending alone never claims confirmed inventory. The notice links to **مركز المزامنة**; dismissing it does not remove the document.

Expand **ملخص أداء الأعمال** on home for charts or open **التقارير والرسوم**. The Arabic client guide explains receiving, supply and navigation. Stock transfers, selected sales deductions, costing, offline grants, API contracts and recovery formats are unchanged.
