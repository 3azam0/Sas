# Operating expenses

Open **المصروفات والتصحيحات**, select the location and choose **إضافة مصروف**. Enter date, reference, searchable category (or add a new category), positive EGP amount, payment method and optional notes. Examples: electricity, phone credit and knife sharpening. Buying inventory belongs in receiving/purchasing; recording it again here would double-count costs. Payment method is a record, not bank settlement.

Accepted entries cannot be edited or deleted. **عكس المصروف** creates a full reversal linked to the original value and records a reason; it can post once. Add the corrected expense separately. Reversals use their own date and cannot precede their source.

The journal supports search, type/status/date filters, sorting, pagination and expense-category chart drill-down. Operational pages use the downloaded snapshot; the dedicated financial report aggregates and pages the complete filtered range on the server, with CSV and cached copies.

**المصروفات والنتيجة التقديرية** shows net sales after dated corrections, net operating expenses, full recipe estimates and estimated result. Missing recipe prices stay incomplete; zero is valid. This is not accounting net profit or proof of actual consumption. Purchases, transfers, production inputs and waste are not deducted again from recipe estimates. Use physical-count reconciliation to prove actual consumption.

The shared forms retain keyboard shortcuts, visible save feedback and mobile scrolling. Offline capture is bounded by the same tenant grant and includes recovery format 18. The journal enters confirmed totals only after acceptance.
